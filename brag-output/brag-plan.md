# TerraOps GTS — Brag Video Plan

**What it is:** TerraOps GTS is a full-stack Angular 19+ fleet and transport management platform for Mining, BTP, and heavy-equipment operators.

**Who it's for:** Operations managers who run fleets of heavy equipment (cranes, bulldozers) and need one place for every contract, compliance check, and invoice.

**What sets it apart:** It goes from "is this crane insured?" to "has the client paid?" without leaving the app — insurance tracking, VGP safety inspections, rentals, missions, and FCFA invoicing all in one dashboard.

**The hook:** Smart notifications alert before an insurance expires or a safety check lapses, so nothing slips through the cracks of a busy site.

**Visual hook:** KPI cards counting up live — revenue, active rentals, unpaid invoices — tells the whole story in four numbers.

**One-line share caption:** TerraOps GTS: from the job site to the invoice, every vehicle, every contract, every FCFA.
